Privacy Policy — Neon Climb

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Neon Climb is developed by Pelagon Games. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with your information.

Summary

Neon Climb does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. The game works entirely offline and requires no account.

Information stored on your device

Neon Climb saves your game progress locally on your device using Android's standard app storage. This includes:

  • High scores, best floor reached and best combo
  • Leaderboard entries you have set (stored on the device, not online)
  • Trails you have unlocked and the one you have equipped
  • Daily mission and achievement progress
  • Your settings

This information never leaves your device. It is not uploaded anywhere, and the developer cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes all of it permanently.

Information collected

None. Neon Climb contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking, and makes no network connections. It does not request your location, your contacts, your camera, your microphone, or your files.

Children

Neon Climb is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no data at all, no personal information is knowingly collected from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Neon Climb adds features that collect data — for example advertising — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the Data safety information on the Google Play listing will be updated to match.
