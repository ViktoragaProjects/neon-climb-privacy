Privacy Policy — Neon Climb

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Neon Climb is developed by Pelagon Games. This policy explains what the game collects, what it does not, and who else receives information when you play.

Summary

Neon Climb does not ask for your name, email or any account of ours. It does show advertisements, and it can connect to Google Play Games. Both of those involve third parties receiving technical information about your device, which is described below.

Information stored on your device

Your progress is saved locally using Android's standard app storage: high scores, best floor and combo, local leaderboard entries, unlocked trails, daily mission and achievement progress, and your settings. Uninstalling the game deletes all of it.

Advertising

Neon Climb shows optional rewarded video advertisements. You are never required to watch one; they are offered in exchange for continuing a run or for a free trail roll, and the game is fully playable without ever watching any.

Advertisements are supplied by a third-party advertising network. To select and measure ads, that network receives:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • technical device information, such as model, operating system version, language and screen size
  • network information, including your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be derived
  • whether an advertisement was shown, watched or dismissed

This information is collected and shared with the advertising network for advertising purposes. Advertisements are mediated by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), and are handled under Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. LevelPlay may source advertisements from other advertising networks, each under its own privacy policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your device, which prevents it being used to build a profile across apps.

Your advertising choice

The first time you open Neon Climb, the game asks whether your advertising ID may be used to personalise the advertisements you choose to watch. Nothing is collected and the advertising network is not started until you have answered. The question is asked of everyone, wherever you are, so the game never needs to work out where you are in order to decide whether to ask.

Whichever way you answer, the game is identical: the same rewards, the same continue offer, the same free trail roll. Declining means the advertisements you see are not personalised.

You can change your answer at any time under Options → DATA → PERSONALISED ADS. Withdrawing is a single tap, exactly like granting.

Google Play Games

Neon Climb can connect to Google Play Games to show global leaderboards. This is optional — the entire game, including its local leaderboards, works without signing in.

If you do sign in, Google provides the game with your Play Games player identifier and display name, and any score you submit is stored on Google's servers so it can appear on the leaderboard. This is handled under Google's privacy policy.

Signing in also turns on cloud save. Your game progress — best scores, unlocked trails, credits, daily missions and achievements — is stored in your own Google account through Play Games Saved Games, so it can be restored on another device or after a reinstall. It is kept in the private app storage of your Google account; Pelagon Games cannot read it. If the cloud copy and the copy on your device disagree, the game shows you both and asks which to keep — it is never decided for you.

Your settings, and your advertising choice, are never uploaded. Those stay on each device.

Information we receive

Pelagon Games operates no servers and receives no personal information about you. We can see aggregate, anonymous figures from Google Play and the advertising network — such as how many people installed the game, or how many advertisements were watched — which cannot identify an individual.

Deleting your data

This section is the deletion request procedure for Neon Climb, published by Pelagon Games. Pelagon Games operates no servers and holds no account for you, so there is no profile of yours for us to delete. What exists is listed below, with the step that removes each part.

1. Your game progress, on your device

Scores, best floor and combo, local leaderboard entries, unlocked trails, credits, daily missions, achievements and settings. This is the copy on the device in your hand; if you use cloud save, see step 4 as well.

To delete: either open Options → DATA → RESET SAVE inside the game, or uninstall Neon Climb. Both remove all of it immediately and permanently. Nothing is retained afterwards.

2. Your Google Play Games data

Only if you chose to sign in: your Play Games player identifier and any scores you submitted to the global leaderboards. This is held by Google, not by us.

To delete: open the Google Play Games app, go to Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, and choose to delete the data for Neon Climb individually or for all games. Google processes the request and its own retention periods apply.

3. Your advertising ID

Only if you allowed personalised advertising: the advertising ID held by the advertising network for ad selection and measurement.

To delete: turn the choice off at any time in Options → DATA → PERSONALISED ADS inside the game, and reset or delete the ID itself in Android's Settings → Privacy → Ads. Resetting issues a new ID and breaks any profile built against the old one. The network's own retention period applies to data already collected; see its policy, linked above.

4. Your cloud save

Only if you chose to sign in: a copy of your game progress held in your Google account through Play Games Saved Games.

To delete: open the Google Play Games app, go to Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, and delete the data for Neon Climb. That removes the cloud copy along with your leaderboard scores. Deleting the cloud copy does not touch the progress on your device — use step 1 for that.

Anything else

Email kostadinovski.v@outlook.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We will reply within 30 days. Because we hold no personal data about you, the reply will usually be to point you at whichever of the three steps above applies.

Children

Neon Climb is not directed at children under 13 and is not distributed through Google Play's family programmes.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what is collected, this policy and the Data safety information on the Google Play listing will both be updated before that version is released.
